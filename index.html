Ask Us Anything

What exactly is an "AI assistant"?

It's a smart chat assistant trained on your business: your services, prices, opening hours and FAQs. It replies to customers on your website, WhatsApp and Instagram, books them in, and passes anything tricky to you.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. We write its personality to match how you talk to customers, whether that's friendly and chatty or short and professional. You approve everything before it goes live.

What if it gets something wrong?

It only answers from the information you've given it. If it isn't sure, it takes the customer's details and hands over to you. We check conversations every month and keep improving it.

Do I need to be good with tech?

Not at all. That's the point of "done for you". We need a 20-minute chat and a few logins, and we handle the rest.

How long does it take to set up?

Most businesses are live within 7 days of sending us their details.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans run month to month with 30 days' notice. Founding clients keep their launch price for as long as they stay.

What about my customers' data and GDPR?

We only collect what's needed to reply to and book your customers, use reputable providers, and can sign a data processing agreement with you. You own your customer data.

Does it work with my booking system or calendar?

It works with Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly and many popular booking tools. If yours isn't listed, ask in your free audit and we'll check.

Can it answer my phone?

Every plan includes missed-call text-back. Full AI phone answering is available as an add-on because it's priced by call minutes. We'll quote it in your audit.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

Use your assistant for 30 days after it goes live. If you're not happy, email us and we'll refund your setup fee in full. Simple.
