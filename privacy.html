Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Owlroo ("we", "us") is a small UK business that builds AI assistants and automations for other businesses. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Owlroo is run by Chris in Mansfield, Nottinghamshire, United Kingdom. Contact: 07520 664498 or chrisfaulkes16@gmail.com. For data protection purposes we are the controller of the data described below.

What we collect

  • Audit and contact requests: your name, business name, email, phone (optional), business type and anything you tell us in the message box.
  • Business outreach: publicly listed business contact details (business name, business email or contact page, business phone) that we find on business websites and directories, so we can contact businesses about our services.
  • Clients: the details needed to set up, bill and support your service.

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this website.

Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To reply to your audit or contact request: steps before entering a contract / legitimate interests.
  • To contact businesses about services relevant to them: legitimate interests (business-to-business marketing). We follow the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, every message tells you who we are, and you can opt out at any time.
  • To provide and bill for our services: contract and legal obligation (for example, keeping accounting records).

Who we share it with

We only share data with the services we use to run the business, such as email, form handling (our audit form is delivered by FormSubmit), website hosting (GitHub Pages) and payment processing. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't go ahead: up to 12 months. Client records: for as long as you're a client plus 6 years for tax purposes. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we keep a minimal record (your email or business name) only so we don't contact you again.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to our use of it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Just email chrisfaulkes16@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
